auto-log In development

Spring Boot 4 · Java 21 · Vue 3 · PostgreSQL · Flyway

A maintenance log for people who do their own work on their cars. Keep a running record of every fuel-up, service and repair across the garage — then hand a buyer the whole history when it's time to sell.

  • The whole garage in one place

    Register each car, then log fuel-ups, service, repairs and modifications with dates, costs, receipts and notes — all on one timeline per vehicle.

  • Miles or kilometres, per car

    Each vehicle records the odometer in whatever unit its cluster reads. Fuel economy between fill-ups, cost per mile and spend by category stay comparable across a mixed garage.

  • Reminders for what's due

    Set a rule once — oil every 5,000 miles — and every car it covers shows how close it is. Logging the work is what clears it.

  • A history sheet for buyers

    Print or save a PDF of a vehicle's full service history, choosing what goes on it: costs, modifications, fuel economy, VIN, plate. What you paid for the car is never included.

edh-budget-brewer In development

Django · DRF · Python 3.13 · PostgreSQL · React

A deckbuilding tool for Magic: The Gathering Commander. Say which commander you're building and how much you want to spend, and it puts together a complete, legal deck that fits the budget. Command line now, web app in progress.

  • A full deck from two inputs

    Pick a commander, set a dollar amount, and all 100 cards come back — lands, ramp, card draw, removal and a curve that actually works — in under a second.

  • Built around your commander

    Cards are chosen to match what the commander does, so a Krenko list comes back full of goblins and a Muldrotha list leans on the graveyard.

  • It spends what you give it

    Set $75 and the deck lands near $75, not far under. Prices come from Scryfall and refresh daily.

  • Paste straight into your deckbuilder

    Export a plain text list that drops into Moxfield or Archidekt, or JSON if you want the raw data.
